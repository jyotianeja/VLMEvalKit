We are given that $\overline{AB}$ is a diameter of the circle, and points $A$, $B$, and $C$ lie on the circle. We are also given that $AC = 8$ inches and $BC = 15$ inches.

Since $\overline{AB}$ is a diameter and point $C$ is on the circle, by **Thales’ Theorem**, triangle $ABC$ is a **right triangle** with the right angle at $C$. That is, $\angle ACB = 90^\circ$.

Therefore, triangle $ABC$ is a right triangle with legs $AC = 8$ and $BC = 15$, and hypotenuse $AB$ (the diameter).

We can find the length of the hypotenuse $AB$ using the Pythagorean theorem:

$$
AB^2 = AC^2 + BC^2 = 8^2 + 15^2 = 64 + 225 = 289
$$

$$
AB = \sqrt{289} = 17 \text{ inches}
$$

Since $AB$ is the diameter, the radius $r$ is half of the diameter:

$$
r = \frac{AB}{2} = \frac{17}{2} = 8.5 \text{ inches}
$$
